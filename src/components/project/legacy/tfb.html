The Challenge

The Gladwin Brothers wanted a visual identity that could hold two ideas at once: a fine-dining restaurant and a fresh, seasonal grocer, sharing one roof. It needed to resonate with their existing clientele while pulling in a new one entirely.

Brand Story

A Farmer Walks Into a Bar…

The Approach

The moodboard leaned into handpainted signage — the emphasis on typography and the craft of its execution. We loved the texture of one weathered old sign in particular, the way age had worn into the letterforms, and borrowed that as inspiration for texture within our own type. The arcs in the composition, both structural and decorative, came from the same source.

The Fat Badger

"Since launching our first restaurant, The Shed, in 2012, we've had the privilege of collaborating with Jason de Villiers. His innovative input is a game-changer for any new business."

Logo Development

A flexible visual system for all applications — the full lockup, a solid reversal, a standalone wordmark, and the badger-head mark on its own, each one built to hold up at any size, from a farm shop sign to a jar label.

Colours

Two shades that reflect the farm-to-fork ethos while drawing from nature: Camo Green captures the earthy tones of fresh produce and the outdoors, while Lichen evokes the soft, mossy greens found growing wild.

Poster & Tote Bag

"Lettuce, Turnip the Beet" — a headline that blends fresh produce with a clever pun, echoing the restaurant's playful blend of natural ingredients and lively atmosphere. The tote bag carries the same spirit: a pesto recipe on one side, the ingredients and tools to make it on the other.

The Outcome

A flexible visual system now runs across signage, bags, and collateral — one identity, doing double duty as both restaurant and grocer, without ever feeling like two brands wearing one badge.
