The Challenge

EARNT already had a visual identity and a genuinely good idea — rewarding people for environmentally conscious acts with exclusive, one-of-a-kind experiences. What it didn't have yet was a way to say that felt as bold as the idea itself.

Brand Story

Natural Resistance

The Approach

Two lines became our compass: the brand line "a revolution in consumption," and the logo rationale, "the world as your playground." We built the visual direction at the intersection of revolution-era poster art, street and activewear culture, and lush, unmistakably green nature — campaign energy, not brochure earnestness.

The River Cafe

EARNT collaborated with the prestigious River Cafe to help clean up the Thames. Each participant was awarded one of these enamel keyrings. The keyring was proof of participation and granted the EARNT member a complimentary drink at every visit.

The Outcome

The direction rolled out across EARNT's website, pitch materials, flyers, and event merchandise — giving a genuinely good cause the cultural pull to match.

Since the repositioning, EARNT has built meaningful collaborations with the likes of Harry Styles, Peloton, Soho House, The River Cafe Cafe and Hagen Coffee.
