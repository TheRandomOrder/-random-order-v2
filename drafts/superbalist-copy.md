# Superbalist — case-study copy draft

For review only. The live case-study copy has not been changed.

## Opening

Six years. One evolving digital ecosystem. From identity to interface, from individual components to a shared system.

## Product journeys

Navigation, filtering and product discovery had to feel like parts of the same experience. The interface gave people a clear path from browsing to purchase while leaving room for the product to grow.

## Identity in use

The S became more than a mark. Its shapes informed icons and visual patterns across the product, giving the interface a recognisable language of its own.

## Stitch

Stitch brought the work into a system: typography, grids, colour, components and interaction patterns. It helped a changing product retain coherence across screens and journeys.

## Looking back

The historical interface designs and source artefacts show the work as it was. Contemporary visual studies revisit that material through a new lens; they should be identified as reconstructions, not presented as original screenshots.
